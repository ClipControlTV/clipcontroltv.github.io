This policy explains how the ClipControlTV Uploader ("the app") handles data. The app is a private tool used only by the ClipControlTV channel operator to publish short videos to ClipControlTV's own social media accounts.

Who uses the app

Only the ClipControlTV channel operator. The app is not available to the public and doesn't collect information from visitors, viewers or other users.

What data the app accesses

When the operator connects ClipControlTV's own accounts, the app receives the following:

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the ClipControlTV channel and to read basic channel and video information (titles, view counts and similar statistics).
  • TikTok: permission to publish videos to the ClipControlTV TikTok account and to read basic account and video information.
  • Facebook (Meta): permission to publish Reels to the ClipControlTV Facebook Page and to read that Page's basic insights.

The app also reads public Twitch stream information and chat messages, solely to find highlight moments.

How the data is used

This data is used only to upload ClipControlTV's videos and to measure how they perform. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't shared with anyone else.

How the data is stored

Access tokens and settings are kept only on the operator's own computer. They aren't uploaded to any server run by ClipControlTV. Short excerpts of stream audio transcripts may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to help judge whether a moment is worth clipping. No account data from Google, TikTok or Meta is sent there.

Google user data

The ClipControlTV Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions
  • Facebook: Settings → Business integrations
