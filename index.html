ClipControlTV is a short-form video channel that posts vertical highlight clips from Twitch live streams to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels.

About the ClipControlTV Uploader

The ClipControlTV Uploader is a private tool used only by the ClipControlTV channel operator. It finds highlight moments in live streams, edits them into short vertical clips and publishes them to ClipControlTV's own accounts on:

  • YouTube (through the YouTube Data API)
  • TikTok (through the TikTok Content Posting API)
  • Facebook Pages (through the Meta Graph API)

The tool is not offered to the public. Nobody else can sign in to it, and it doesn't post to anyone else's accounts.
